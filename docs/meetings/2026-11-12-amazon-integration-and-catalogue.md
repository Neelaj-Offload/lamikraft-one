Product catalogue aur variations

* Products ki Excel nahi hai, sirf catalogue hai; products ek-ek karke, variation wise daalne honge
* Ek unit teen factors se banta hai: design number, decor number aur size
   * Example: chopping board CB01 se CB26, har ek mein 4-5 size (small, medium, large, XL)
   * Alag decor (jaise 892 aur 959) se alag SKU banega
* Sizes variation rahenge par stock ke liye ek unit maana jayega; best structure abhi final nahi


Seller Central aur Unicommerce setup

* Platform logins ke passwords wali Excel sheet share ki gayi; Amazon Seller Central (sellercentral.amazon.in) backend khola gaya
* Unicommerce inventory management software hai: ek jagah stock update hota hai aur sab connected platforms par sync hota hai
* ERP mein bhi yahi inventory sync banana hai, par ye utna easy nahi lagta
* Developer section mein "LamiKraft ERP" app shuru kiya; production ke liye developer profile approval chahiye
